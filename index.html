Core Lessons

Read these before opening the Plan Builder.

BEGINNER FRIENDLY
1. What is a stock?A stock is a small ownership slice of a company. When you buy shares, you're betting the company's value rises over time, or that it pays you dividends.
2. How to read a candlestickEach candle shows the open, high, low, and close price for a period. Green usually means it closed higher than it opened; red means lower.
3. Support and resistanceSupport is a price level where buying has historically stepped in. Resistance is where selling has capped rallies.
4. Order typesMarket order fills immediately. Limit order only fills at your price or better. Stop order triggers a market order once a price is hit.
5. Risk, reward, and position sizeMost professional traders risk only 1–2% of their account per trade. ORBIT's Plan Builder flags any plan risking more than that.
6. Trading psychologyDecide your entry, stop, and target before you enter — not while emotions are running. Journal every trade.
