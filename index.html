BEGINNER FRIENDLY
1. WHAT IS A STOCK?
A stock is a small ownership slice of a company. When you buy shares, you're betting the company's value rises over time, or that it pays you dividends. Price moves based on supply, demand, and expectations about the company's future.
2. HOW TO READ A CANDLESTICK
Each candle shows the open, high, low, and close price for a time period. A green candle means price closed higher than it opened; red means it closed lower. The thin lines above and below the body are wicks — the highest and lowest prices reached in that period.
3. SUPPORT AND RESISTANCE
Support is a price level where buying pressure has historically stepped in, stopping declines. Resistance is where selling pressure has capped rallies. Traders often anchor stops just below support (long) or above resistance (short) instead of using round numbers.
4. ORDER TYPES
Market order — buy or sell immediately at the best available price. Limit order — only fills at your chosen price or better. Stop order — becomes a market order once a trigger price is hit, commonly used for stop-losses.
5. RISK, REWARD, AND POSITION SIZE
Most professional traders risk only 1–2% of their account per trade. ORBIT's Plan Builder calculates this automatically — enter your account size and max loss, and it flags any plan risking more than 2%. Also check the breakeven win rate: a 1:2 risk/reward setup only needs to win about 33% of the time to break even.
6. TRADING PSYCHOLOGY
Decide your entry, stop, and target before you enter — not while the position is open and emotions are running. Journaling every trade, which ORBIT now auto-logs from your saved plans, is one of the most consistently cited habits among profitable traders.
