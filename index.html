Core Concepts

6 MODULES
01 — What is a stock?

A small ownership slice of a company. You're betting its value rises over time, or that it pays dividends.

02 — Reading a candlestick

Each candle shows open, high, low, close for a period. Green closed higher than it opened; red closed lower.

03 — Support and resistance

Support: where buying has historically stepped in. Resistance: where selling has capped rallies.

04 — Order types

Market: fills now. Limit: fills at your price or better. Stop: becomes a market order once triggered.

05 — Risk and position size

Professionals risk 1–2% per trade. The Plan Builder flags anything above that threshold.

06 — Psychology

Decide entry, stop, target before you enter — not while a position is open and emotions run.
